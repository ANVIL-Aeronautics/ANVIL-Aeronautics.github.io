Builder Story

Driven by a intense focus on learning and execution from an early age.

  • Spent consecutive years ranked near the top of the entire school in middle school, eventually securing the first rank through absolute grind.
  • Failing to enter a science high school became the first major turning point, shattering conventional elite student conformity and forging a desire to prove things directly through physical execution.

2. Independent Curiosity & The New Worldview

Obsessed with the physical constraints governing motion, velocity, and trajectories.

  • Self-studied aerospace mechanics, fluid dynamics, and cosmic expansion models outside the standard high school curriculum.
  • Utilized a gap year to change deeply: mathematically modeled fluid variables, adopted a first-principles framework, and completely mastered working English independently to interface with global tech networks.
  • Grew dedicated to the concepts of open societies, national security, and the idea that technology must serve human freedom.

3. The Realities of Validation & Teams

Discovering that logical correctness requires undeniable physical proof to survive.

  • At Seoul National University, pointed out critical engineering club anomalies that were ignored—and later vindicated when those exact issues forced catastrophic failures in competition.
  • Founded an independent team to build a two-stage rocket system despite academic skepticism, managing rigorous schedules, budget allocations, and engineering the payload separation mechanism to achieve a major prize.
  • Obtained commercial heavy driving credentials and traffic accident appraiser expertise to master ground vehicle dynamics and collision analytics under extreme constraints.

4. The Valley of Death & Rebirth

Surviving the collapse of a hardware initiative to emerge as a disciplined builder.

  • Attempted a deep-tech aerospace hardware startup in 2025 and hit the absolute wall: co-founder friction, funding depletion, team disintegration, and severe burnout leading to a 13kg weight loss.
  • Rebuilt an internal operating system through faith in Jesus Christ, decoupling personal validation from immediate engineering outcomes.
  • Demonstrated extreme structural agility by joining an ideathon outside his domain, synthesizing a complex microbial wastewater business architecture in 30 minutes, and delivering a pitch that secured 1st place.

5. Strategic Alignment

Validating technical intuition against global defense standards.

  • Pushed through 11 iterations of a custom ornithopter platform, treating repeated structural failures purely as heuristic physical data.
  • At ADEX 2025, discovered a newly displayed compact payload concept from a major defense firm that mirrored his exact unexecuted design from 10 months prior, proving his technical instinct was highly aligned with the future battlefield.
